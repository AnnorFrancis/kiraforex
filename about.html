Helping Africans trade better

Kira teaches the market the way she trades it: structure first, risk before reward, and the patience to wait for the setup. More than 460,000 people follow her free forecasts and daily posts across four platforms.

She founded Kiraforex Trading Academy to give African traders a serious, structured place to learn, and has taken it on tour across Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Uganda, South Africa, the Emirates and Europe. In 2025 she spoke at Traders Fair South Africa, was at the Affiliate and Influencers Summit in Dubai, and received a SmartVision award.
